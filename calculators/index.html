Calculators

Interactive tools to help you plan your journey to financial freedom

In the Financial Freedom Roadmap section I talked about the importance of moving away from effort income into leveraged income and passive income.

There was also the MOB Calculator (Maximise, Optimise, Build), which showed how, as you increase income and optimise lifestyle costs, the surplus can flow into investing in yourself and investing in assets. I call this the investment engine, and it’s this engine that will power your financial freedom.

I have created some useful calculators to help you visualise the power of this method. They each work slightly differently and you will likely have a favourite.

Each calculator can be saved and shared with your own data, so you can come back to it anytime.

01

How Much Is Enough?

This shows the size of the investment engine you will need to fund the life you want. Simply enter a few details:

  • your desired monthly income at today’s purchasing power (the calculator adjusts for inflation)
  • how many years until you want to stop earning effort income
  • your current liquid assets
  • the leveraged income you expect to have by then

The calculator then works out the future income you’ll need, how much is enough using generally recognised safe withdrawal rates, what you still need to save, and how much to save each month to reach your goal.

Calculator How Much Is Enough?
02

Liquid Asset Forecaster

This shows how your liquid assets can grow over time with the power of regular saving and compounding. Simply enter a few details:

  • your current liquid assets
  • your monthly contribution
  • expected investment return
  • your time horizon

The calculator then works out your overall growth, and the annual and monthly income this will generate using generally recognised safe withdrawal rates. The expected income is also provided at today’s purchasing power so it has real meaning.

Calculator Liquid Asset Forecaster
03

The Freedom Formula

This third and final calculator is the holy grail. It shows your freedom runway. Simply enter a few details:

  • your leveraged income
  • your lifestyle expenses
  • your liquid assets
  • expected investment return

The calculator then works out how long you can last on your leveraged income and the passive income from your investment engine. If your combined income covers your lifestyle expenses, the runway never ends and you’re free.

You can also set a minimum liquid assets level, so the calculator won’t take your pot below a figure you’re comfortable with.
